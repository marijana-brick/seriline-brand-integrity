## Verified company context
- Brand/company: Seriline / Seriline AB, Swedish company focused on identity and access management, with proprietary software, web solutions, hardware and services.
- Company framing: manage people, credentials, permissions and assets across lifecycles; simplify operational complexity into usable, secure workflows.
- Audiences evident from the site: organizations, companies, municipalities, public-sector entities, security/IT and facilities stakeholders; relevant end users also include administrators, reception teams, employees and visitors. Specific persona prioritization must be confirmed per project.
- Offer names: Serix IAM, Serix Online, Serix ID, Serix IoT, Visitor, Cidron readers, PhotoID systems, terminals, RFID credentials, cards, FIDO2 products. Treat product names and capabilities as exact, current claims to verify before publishing.
- Core themes stated publicly: confidentiality/integrity, secure identity handling, automation, precision, efficiency and user-friendly processes.
- Languages: English and Swedish public site variants; request locale or follow brief. Do not translate registered product names.

## Character — WORKING DIRECTION inferred from public materials (not official brand rules)
1. **Assured, not theatrical:** security and trust from clarity, documented capabilities and care; no dramatic hacker imagery, exaggerated threat rhetoric or fabricated certification badges.
2. **Practical, not abstract:** show real credentials, readers, terminals, dashboards and actual workplace tasks instead of decorative AI imagery.
3. **Systematic, not sterile:** consistent alignment, hierarchy, product photography and restrained color create recognition without generic SaaS-card layouts.

## Voice and editorial direction — inferred, not official
- Write with measured B2B confidence. State the user problem and actual mechanism before benefits.
- Favor concrete terms: identities, credentials, access rights, permissions, visitors, lifecycle, card readers, integration, issuance, traceability — only where technically accurate.
- Explain how processes change: e.g., 'Manage credentials and permissions across connected systems' rather than 'Unlock seamless next-generation identity experiences'.
- Avoid empty hype: 'revolutionary', 'game-changing', 'unleash', 'elevate', 'future-proof' without support, 'military-grade', 'unhackable', '100% secure', and generic AI hero slogans.
- Avoid inventing SLA, GDPR compliance, audit certification scope, encryption specifications, or customer outcomes. Privacy/security/legal claims require approved evidence and the right reviewer.
- Sample directions (ILLUSTRATIVE, NOT official copy):
  - UI: 'Assign access', 'Review credentials', 'Visitor registration', 'Issue ID card'
  - Sales: 'Centralize identity and credential management' / 'Connect employee records to access workflows'
  - Technical: 'The administrator updates an employee's access rights in the management system.'
